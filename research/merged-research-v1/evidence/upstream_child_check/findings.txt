L-meta-7545 remains a conjecture in the official ledger. The merged reduction
R_meta_2631_731 from PR 10278 uses this claim as its privateCase hypothesis.
The pull request body mentions a locally tested proof without linking its source.
The local checkouts and public contributor branch search yielded no exact proof.

The proposition in L-meta-7545.statement.txt is the ledger statement. Its UTF-8
bytes, excluding the trailing file newline, match the published SHA-256
38cf323d98042d209d1dab50c499e68983f1ba927fb6f2d73797c8526bb4fa92.
PostedStatement.lean imports the statement's defining module. Lean 4.36.0
timed out after 55 seconds with the umbrella PEqualNP.Defs import and after
90 seconds with the narrower NestedCoverCircuit import. Neither attempt
reached an elaboration verdict. Elaborating a proposition checks its type
and supplies no proof.

For a clean least gate list, adapt EdgeFnAnd.and_core. Replace the private
operand gate with NOT x. Under x=false, the AND output stays false and other
wires keep their values. Charge the private gate, the AND, the second reader
of x, and a later reader of the AND. The second reader cannot be the private
operand or the AND's later reader, by Readers.Least.no_tri. The existence of
the later reader follows from minimality and non-constancy under the
restriction. For the graph function, an edge survives x=false: if a known
edge meets x, its other endpoint has degree three and hence a neighbor
different from x. Compare the all-false assignment with one setting both
ends of that surviving edge true. The private operand cannot be the output
in a least circuit, because the later AND would then be past the output
and removable. The exact posted theorem additionally requires preserving the
reader and private-operand premises when moving from its arbitrary minimal
circuit to the clean representation. Existence of some clean least circuit
alone does not preserve those premises. This is a proof plan.
