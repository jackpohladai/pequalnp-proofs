For F_k = AND_i (x_i OR y_i), split the ordered input after all x variables
and before all y variables. The truth table at that cut is a 2^k by 2^k
matrix over GF(2). Its entry is 1 exactly when each coordinate pair has at
least one true bit. Therefore the matrix is the k-fold Kronecker power of
B=[[0,1],[1,1]]. The inverse of B is C=[[1,1],[1,0]]. Their tensor powers
are inverses, hence the cut matrix has rank exactly 2^k.

Any exact matrix-chain representation with intermediate dimension d at
this cut gives a factorization K=L*R through d coordinates. Rank(K) <= d,
so d>=2^k. This is a lower bound for this exact fixed-order representation.
It is not a lower bound for deciding SAT. This family is easy to satisfy
and is also 2-SAT; a direct witness, a 2-SAT fast path, another order or a
different representation can bypass it. With the alternating order
x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2,..., the same product has bounded intermediate width.

RankObstruction.lean formalizes recursive bit tuples, the kernel and its
explicit inverse, both inverse products, dimension 2^k, full rank, and the
factorization width bound. Its Boolean jointIndicator states exactly the
coordinatewise AND-of-ORs function; kernel_entry connects it to the matrix.

Feedback: a generator proposing fixed-order exact rank compression should
state and prove a small-rank invariant for every allowed input, or expose
the width growth and return UNKNOWN honestly. Avoid claiming that passing
this easy family after adding a 2-SAT fast path supplies a general bound.
The remaining goal is a different universally controlled representation,
an order-selection theorem with total cost, or a different algorithm.
